How it works

    We talk about your business and what's wasting your time. You then get one clear page: the problem, what the first version would do, the total monthly cost, and our honest opinion on whether it's worth it.

  2. Before we build

    Together we pin down the scope of the first version: the screens, the data, your business rules and where the tool will be hosted. The clock starts once that scope is approved.

  3. 2 to 8 weeks

    We build the tool on the approved scope. You use it for real, with your teams and your data, whether it is a web app or a native app.

  4. ZAR 9,900 excl. VAT per month

    Your tool evolves with you. New features, fixes, details: everything goes through your request list, with no quote each time.

Your questions, answered.

I am a…

How is this possible at this price?

Artificial intelligence now writes a large part of the code. So our time goes to what matters: understanding your business, answering you and checking every delivery.

Can I really ask for anything, even big projects?

You'll be surprised by how much we can do with our plan. That said, if your project is especially ambitious and our development pace turns out not to be enough, we can put together a custom offer, for a custom tool (as always).

What kind of tools can you build?

Internal tools (client tracking, quotes, job scheduling, stock management, dashboards) as well as tools for your customers (customer portal, appointment booking, online ordering).

Is my business big enough?

Yes, from just a few people. If a task costs you hours every week, a tool built for you pays for itself quickly. That's what the free audit is for: we tell you honestly whether it's worth it, even if the answer is no.

Do we have to cancel our other software?

No. Your tool can replace a spreadsheet or software that no longer suits you, but it can also complement what you already use and connect to it where possible. We sort it out together during the audit.

What do you need from our side?

Someone available about an hour a week to test and approve what we deliver.

How is this different from Claude, Copilot or ChatGPT?

Those assistants help you produce text or code, but they don't deliver a finished tool that's tested, hosted and maintained. We use them to move fast, and we stay accountable for the result: one person who understands your business, answers you and commits to dates.

How do you stop the AI making mistakes?

AI writes the code, but nothing reaches you unchecked. Every feature goes through automated tests, then a manual review, and only goes live once approved.

How do we ask for changes?

However suits you: a WhatsApp message, an email, or straight from your dashboard, where you follow what's delivered, in progress and planned. Bugs and tweaks are handled within 2 business hours; new features get a date announced in advance.

What happens if something breaks?

You tell us, and it's fixed within 2 business hours. And the person who built your tool handles it: no ticket passed from hand to hand.

What happens if I cancel the subscription?

It's up to you. You can switch to a maintenance-only plan, at ZAR 990 excl. VAT per month, hosting not included: your tool stays maintained, with no new requests. Or you leave, and you keep your data and your tool: we hand over the code and the database, or help you install them on your side.

Where is my data stored?

Which technologies do you use?

We keep a regular watch on development tools in the age of AI, so at the audit we can offer you the best-performing, most reliable combination available. Depending on your needs, that's custom code, or Bubble if you want to be able to edit the tool yourself.
