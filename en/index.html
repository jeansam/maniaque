How it works

  1. 45 minutes

    We talk about your business and what's wasting your time. You then get one clear page: the problem, what the first version would do, the total monthly cost, and our honest opinion on whether it's worth it.

    Book my free audit

  2. Before we build

    Together we pin down the scope of the first version: the screens, the data, your business rules and where the tool will be hosted. The clock starts once that scope is approved.

  3. 2 to 8 weeks

    We build the tool on the approved scope. You use it for real, with your teams and your data, whether it is a web app or a native app.

  4. €490 excl. VAT per month

    Your tool evolves with you. New features, fixes, details: everything goes through your request list, with no quote each time.

Frequently asked questions

How is this possible at this price?

Artificial intelligence now writes a large part of the code. So our time goes to what matters: understanding your business, answering you and checking every delivery.

Can I really ask for anything, even big projects?

You'll be surprised by how much we can do with our plan. That said, if your project is especially ambitious and our development pace turns out not to be enough, we can put together a custom offer, for a custom tool (as always).

What happens if I cancel the subscription?

You keep your data and your tool. We hand over the code and the database, or help you install them on your side.

Where is my data stored?

We discuss it together during the scoping phase. There are hosting options in Europe and in France, and we adapt to your needs.

Which technologies do you use?

We keep a regular watch on development tools in the age of AI, so at the audit we can offer you the best-performing, most reliable combination available. Depending on your needs, that's custom code, or Bubble if you want to be able to edit the tool yourself.

What kind of tools can you build?

Internal tools (client tracking, quotes, job scheduling, stock management, dashboards) as well as tools for your customers (customer portal, appointment booking, online ordering).

What do you need from our side?

Someone available about an hour a week to test and approve what we deliver.

Let's talk about what bugs you every day.

45 minutes, free and with no commitment. You leave with an honest opinion, even if it's to tell you no.
