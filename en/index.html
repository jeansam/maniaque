Frequently asked questions

How is this possible at this price?

Artificial intelligence now writes a large part of the code. So our time goes to what matters: understanding your business, answering you and checking every delivery.

Can I really ask for anything, even big projects?

Yes. A layout detail or a complete module, your list has no limit. Bugs and tweaks are handled within 2 business hours. New features go one at a time, in the order you choose: their timing depends on their size, because each one is fully tested before going live. You always know the date in advance.

What happens if I cancel the subscription?

You keep your data and your tool. With Bubble, everything is already on your account. With custom code, we hand over the code and the database, or help you install them on your side.

Where is my data stored?

In Europe. A GDPR-compliant data processing agreement is attached to your contract.

What kind of tools can you build?

Internal tools (client tracking, quotes, job scheduling, stock management, dashboards) as well as tools for your customers (customer portal, appointment booking, online ordering).
